Erasmus+ youth exchange · 22 – 29 June 2025

Young people: develop your entrepreneurial spirit in rural areas!

32 young people from five European countries gathered in Nantes to turn entrepreneurship into an answer to youth unemployment in rural areas.

32participants aged 18–30
5countries
8days
5partner organisations

Why this project

Fewer jobs, less training, more departures

In Europe’s rural areas, young people struggle more to find work than in cities: few openings, limited access to training, depopulation. The gap is even wider for young women, often confined to a handful of low-paid, insecure sectors.

Yet rural youth have ideas and energy. This project set out to foster entrepreneurial spirit so as to create local jobs and make people want to stay, or come back.

Objectives

  • Identify the challenges and strengths of rural employment initiatives across European countries.
  • Train young people, through non-formal learning, to design and run an entrepreneurial project in their community.
  • Create a space to exchange knowledge, experience and good practice.
  • Share information on European funding for entrepreneurial projects.
  • Build a lasting network between European organisations.

The programme

Five key stages

Workshops in the morning, field work in the afternoon, a debrief every evening.

Stage 1Welcome and introduction

  • Welcome and welcome kit
  • Ice-breaker and participants’ expectations
  • Opening workshop: the challenges of rural entrepreneurship
  • “NGO Market”: each organisation introduces itself

Stage 2Models of rural entrepreneurship

  • Case study: innovative initiatives
  • Field visit to Pronatura in Rezé
  • Workshop: entrepreneurship support ecosystems

Stage 3Building skills

  • Workshop: creating strategic partnerships
  • Case study: setting up a community project
  • Visit to 23 Degrés in Nantes: innovative farming

Stage 4Inclusion and interculturality

  • Workshop: including women and young people in projects
  • Brainstorm: intercultural and inclusive solutions
  • Intercultural evening and discovering Nantes

Stage 5Closing and next steps

  • Collaborative action planning
  • Evaluation and Youthpass certificates

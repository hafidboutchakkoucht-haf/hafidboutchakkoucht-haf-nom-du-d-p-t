Who we are

A non-profit for those Europe has not reached yet

Cross Borders is a non-profit association based in Rezé, on the doorstep of Nantes. Our belief: one week spent with young people from other countries can change a life path.

Our mission

Unlocking young people’s potential, beyond borders

We design and host European youth projects, mainly under the Erasmus+ programme: youth exchanges, training courses and partnerships between organisations.

Our work is aimed first at young people with fewer opportunities: those who, for social, economic or geographical reasons, rarely get to go abroad. In every project, each partner organisation commits to including young people in that situation.

We work on concrete, place-based topics: youth entrepreneurship in rural areas, the socio-economic inclusion of women, European citizenship.

Our five pillars

What guides every project

✈️

Mobility

Going abroad, hosting, travelling differently: mobility as a learning experience, with a preference for greener ways to travel.

🗳️

Citizenship

Giving young people the means to understand Europe, speak up and act in their community.

💡

Entrepreneurship

From idea to project: case studies, visits to local initiatives, action plans built as a team.

🫱🏽‍🫲🏾

Inclusion

Gender balance, participation of young people with fewer opportunities, all language levels welcome.

🇪🇺

European cooperation

A network of partner organisations sharing practices, ideas and projects.

Our method: non-formal learning

Learning by doing, in a group, outside the classroom: ice-breakers, workshops, field visits, daily debriefs.

Our commitments

What you can expect from us

  1. No hidden costs

    In our Erasmus+ exchanges, accommodation, meals and activities are covered by the programme.

  2. European recognition

    Every participant receives a Youthpass certificate describing the skills gained.

  3. Protected data

    We apply the GDPR: your data is used only to run the project and is never sold.

  4. Shared results

    Participants and partners share what they learned in their communities and networks.
